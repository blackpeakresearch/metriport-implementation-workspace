PROPOSED OWNERSHIP · FOR DISCUSSION

Making customer launches
repeatable.

Shaunak Mali · Metriport

The opportunity I would like to explore

Metriport’s Sollis Health story shows the value of making outside records available inside the clinician’s existing workflow. The question I would bring to the team is how to make those customer launches easier to repeat as requirements vary across accounts.

I would own a small set of strategic launches and turn what we learn into shared implementation standards, clearer product decisions, and a delivery-capacity plan. The intended result is less repeated work between customers and a clearer route from a working integration to customer acceptance. I would first validate where that work is needed with Customer Success and Engineering.

What I would own

  • Selected launches: agree what success means with each customer, own the delivery plan and escalations, and stay hands-on through acceptance.
  • Patterns across accounts: identify recurring blockers, build and test reusable integration and validation patterns, and recommend which requests merit product investment versus account-specific work.
  • Delivery capacity: make engineering effort and unresolved dependencies visible; propose staffing or process changes where the evidence supports them.

Alongside selected launches, I would own the improvement plan across accounts: which recurring problems to solve once, what the shared approach should be, and whether it works on the next implementation. Within agreed capacity, I would own launch priorities and delivery standards, with product investment and staffing decisions agreed with the existing leaders.

The role I’m exploring is VP, Special Projects. I would want the scope and decision rights to match that responsibility.

Why I am proposing this

At KarmaCheck, I cofounded the company, wrote the original platform, won and onboarded the first customers, and recruited the initial engineering team. That combination of building, customer delivery, and team formation is the experience I would bring to this role.

A first assignment after joining

Choose one upcoming customer workflow with the existing team. I would bring back an agreed workflow and acceptance checklist, a demonstrated integration path in an approved environment, and a readiness review with named owners for unresolved issues. The checks would cover source traceability, incomplete records, failure recovery, and handoff ownership. Clinical interpretation remains with the customer’s clinicians.

The accompanying build shows how I approach the workflow: make the record understandable, let the reviewer inspect its source, and keep unresolved work visible. It is an independent synthetic demonstration, not a production connector or evidence of customer outcomes.

What would make the work viable

An executive sponsor, an agreed boundary with Customer Success and Engineering, access to the selected account’s approved implementation context, and named engineering and customer counterparts. Before starting, we would agree engineering time, the customer’s acceptance owner, who resolves priority conflicts, and any incremental budget. I am not assuming a new team or authority over the product roadmap.

We would track elapsed time from agreed kickoff to customer acceptance, engineering effort per launch, and recurring blockers. Customer and access delays would be identified separately. Initial cases would establish a baseline, not prove a conversion or revenue improvement.

The decision now

Could we use a conversation with a founder and the customer-delivery lead to look at one representative launch and decide whether you need this ownership across accounts? That would make the scope concrete before either side commits to a role design.
